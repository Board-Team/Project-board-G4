## 📖 Overview

**Ink & Pages** is a modern, responsive e-commerce web application designed to supply students, teachers, writers, and book lovers with premium educational materials. From classic literature boxsets and comprehensive programming guides to premium fountain pens and desk organizers, our platform offers a streamlined, user-friendly shopping experience.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   🌓 **Theme Customization:** Built-in Light and Dark mode toggle for optimal viewing comfort.
*   🛒 **Dynamic Shopping Cart:** Real-time cart updates, price calculations, and item management.
*   📦 **Categorized Catalog:** Browse over 500+ items across Books, Pens, Notebooks, Stationery, and Gifts.
*   📱 **Responsive UI/UX:** Fully optimized for desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
*   📰 **Reading Corner Blog:** Integrated section for study tips, stationery guides, and organizational advice.
